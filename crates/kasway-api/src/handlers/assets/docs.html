What Kasway does

Kasway sits between your store and the Kaspa network. It turns a crypto payment into an order status your team can understand and act on.

↯

Request payment

Create a payment request for an order, including the amount the customer needs to pay.

✦

Guide checkout

Show the customer where to send the Kaspa payment and keep the checkout state clear.

⌁

Confirm payment

Watch the Kaspa network and match the incoming payment to the correct order.

▣

Keep records

Store payment history, status changes, alerts, and reports for later review.

How it works

Use this flow when adding Kasway to a store, checkout page, or merchant dashboard.

1

Set up the merchant

Add the store profile, payment address, checkout redirect, and notification settings.

2

Create the order payment

Tell Kasway the order amount. Kasway returns payment details for checkout.

3

Show checkout

Your customer sees the payment instructions and sends Kaspa from their wallet.

4

Match the payment

Kasway watches for the payment and connects it to the correct order.

5

Update the order

When the payment is valid, Kasway marks the order so your team can fulfill it.

6

Review later

Use alerts and reports to answer payment questions after checkout.

Key records

These are the records Kasway keeps so payments stay understandable.

Business

Merchant

The business accepting Kaspa payments.

Security

Access key

A private key your system uses to call Kasway safely.

Order

Invoice

The order payment request and its current status.

Public

Checkout

The customer-facing page or flow used to pay.

Network

Observation

A payment Kasway sees on the Kaspa network.

Money

Credit

The amount Kasway matched to an invoice.

Records

Report

A downloadable view for finance and support.

Sync

Notification

A message sent when payment status changes.
